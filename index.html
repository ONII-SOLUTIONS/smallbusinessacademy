Who We Are & Philosophy

About Small Business Academy

Small Business Academy (registered as G-Stuff Trading Pty Ltd) is a South African skills development institution specializing in artisan training, trade testing, entrepreneurship development, occupational health and safety (OHS) training, and skills development practitioner programs.

Operating from our centralized campus at 127 Johannes Nkosi Street in Durban CBD, we bridge the critical shortage of certified tradesmen in South Africa by combining rigorous theoretical learning with extensive hands-on workshop experience.

Our Impactful Approach

Competence = Technical Artisan Trade Skills + Entrepreneurship Capability. Developing skilled artisans and sustainable enterprise leaders.

Our Strategic Vision

To empower historically disadvantaged communities and drive economic growth in South Africa through accredited artisan and entrepreneurship training.

Core Organizational Values

  • Empowerment: Equipping learners with practical skills and confidence.
  • Excellence: Highest standards in training, assessment, and service delivery.
  • Integrity: Honesty, transparency, fairness, and accountability.
  • Quality Workmanship: Promoting pride in skilled craftsmanship.
  • Entrepreneurial Spirit: Driving innovation and enterprise creation.

2. Occupational Health & Safety (OHS)

Physical Attendance Only
  • SHE Representative Training (Safety, Health & Environment)
  • First Aid Levels 1, 2, and 3
  • Scaffolding Safety & Inspection
  • Working at Heights & Confined Spaces
