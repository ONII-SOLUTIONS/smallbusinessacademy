Company Background & Philosophy

About Small Business Academy

Small Business Academy (SBA) was established to directly respond to the youth unemployment crisis by bridging the gap between technical artisan trade skills and viable business management capabilities.

Our Philosophy

Competence = Artisan Trade and Construction Skills + Entrepreneurship. Shifting minds from crisis to sustainable economic opportunity.

Our Strategic Mission

To deliver SETA and QCTO accredited, internationally benchmarked solutions that empower black youth, artisans, and emerging contractors.

Core Organizational Values

  • Treat all stakeholders with respect and dignity.
  • Honesty and integrity in all business and training conduct.
  • Strict client confidentiality and privacy protection.
  • Employee development and welfare prioritized at all times.
  • Good corporate governance and total workplace equity.
